# R5 return — DQ250 (0D9) transmission: full internal breakdown

Deep-research return received 2026-08-14 (operator-run task, delivered as
PDF; transcribed here). Scope: 2014-plate VW Golf R Mk7 (pre-facelift,
CJXC 300 PS, 4Motion), ~92k miles.

**Scope note on sourcing:** The DQ250 architecture is identical between the
FWD "02E" and the AWD "0D9" housing families; VW documents the internals in
SSP 308 (written around the 02E). Where a fact is verified only against 02E
parts or the older application, it is marked **[02E-verified]**.
Golf-R-specific 0D9 facts are marked **[0D9]**. Source categories:
CAT=dealer catalogue, WSM=workshop manual, SSP=self-study programme,
RT=Ross-Tech, FORUM, VENDOR.

## TL;DR

- This car's gearbox is a **BorgWarner-developed DQ250 six-speed wet
  dual-clutch DSG in the AWD "0D9" housing**, fitted to pre-facelift
  (2014–2016) Golf R/S3 with a front-mounted **bevel box (angle drive)**
  taking drive rearward to the Haldex; the code letters actually used on
  these cars are **PUR, QSP and PZS** and the transmission part number is in
  the **0D9 300 012 L** family.
- At ~92k miles the two service-critical realities are: **DSG oil + filter
  is due every 40,000 miles (approx. 4 years)** — this car should be on its
  3rd change — and the two classic DQ250 wear-outs are **clutch-pack
  shudder/judder (often the odd-gear K1 pack)** and **mechatronic pressure
  faults**; a clutch or mechatronic swap on this UDS-protocol MQB box
  **mandates a VCDS/ODIS basic-setting (clutch adaptation) plus a defined
  test drive**.
- The DQ250 tolerates the R's 380 Nm because it is an **oil-bath (wet)
  multi-plate design** (K1 = odd gears + reverse, K2 = even gears; 9
  friction discs / 11 steel plates OEM) that carries heat away in the same
  fluid the mechatronic pumps — but **BorgWarner rates the clutch module to
  exactly 380 Nm max transferable engine torque**, which is why remapped
  R/S3 boxes are the ones that fail/"crack".

## How this system works (narrative)

The DQ250 is effectively **two manual gearboxes in one case**, each fed by
its own wet clutch, sharing one bell-housing, one differential and one
electro-hydraulic brain (the mechatronic). Torque enters through a
**dual-mass flywheel** onto the twin wet clutch pack. **Clutch K1 drives
the outer, hollow input shaft and carries the odd gears (1st, 3rd, 5th)
plus reverse; clutch K2 drives the inner, solid input shaft running through
the middle of the hollow one and carries the even gears (2nd, 4th, 6th).**
Because one clutch's gearset can be pre-selected while the other is
driving, the box "hands over" from K1 to K2 (and back) by releasing one wet
clutch while applying the other — giving the near-seamless,
torque-uninterrupted shift. [Maktrans]

Both input shafts drive **two output (pinion) shafts**. Crucially for this
build: **output shaft 1 carries gears 1-2-3-4 and output shaft 2 carries
gears 5-6 and reverse, and the two shafts use different final-drive
ratios** (~4.77 on the 1–4 shaft, ~3.44 on the 5/6/R shaft) — so the DQ250
effectively has two final drives feeding one crown wheel/differential.

Everything hydraulic comes from the **mechatronic unit**: an internal pump
pressurises the circuit; **two large pressure-regulator solenoids (N215 for
K1, N216 for K2)** modulate clutch apply pressure, while smaller
pressure/shift solenoids drive the **four hydraulic selector-fork
pistons**. The same fluid **cools the wet clutches** — oil is pumped
through the clutch pack to carry away the heat of slipping engagement,
which is why a wet DSG survives launches and 380 Nm where a dry DQ200 would
cook. On the AWD car, the **bevel box (angle drive)** bolts to the
transaxle and turns drive 90° to the propshaft, sending it back to the rear
Haldex coupling (the Haldex itself is a later brief).

## Gearbox identification [0D9]

- **Designation:** DQ250, 6-speed wet DSG. FWD applications use parts
  prefix **02E**; AWD (4Motion/quattro) applications use **0D9**. The Mk7
  Golf R is the 0D9 AWD variant. [VENDOR gearboxlist; RT wiki]
- **Code letters fitted to 2014–2016 pre-facelift Golf R / S3 (CJXB 280 PS
  / CJXC 300 PS): PUR, QSP, PZS.** [CAT oemVWshop lists 0D9 300 012 L
  fitting CJXB 05/2013–05/2014 and CJXC 05/2013–08/2016, PR-code G0R
  "6-speed automatic transmission for four-wheel drive", codes PUR/QSP/PZS;
  corroborated VENDOR performancespareswarehouse (QSP), redlineautoparts +
  vdladelaide (PZS)]
- **Transmission part-number family: 0D9 300 012 L**
  (siblings/supersessions 0D9 300 011 R, 0D9 300 014 Q, 0D9 300 014 M,
  various -X/-V suffixes). [CAT oemVWshop; VENDOR uspmotorsports; FORUM
  ross-tech scan showing SW 0D9 300 014 M]
- **Later (facelift) codes for contrast:** SKX (2017), SWQ (2018–19). A
  2016 US car has been seen coded PZS. Codes govern ratios/mechatronic
  software; a used box must match the code or be reflashed. [FORUM
  volkswagenforum, ebay]
- **Source disagreement to flag:** A real Mk7 R VCDS scan shows mechatronic
  **HW: 02E 927 770 AS, Component "DQ250-6A MQB", SW 0D9 300 014 M** [FORUM
  ross-tech]. Vendor teardown listings for a PZS Golf R box instead cite
  mechatronic **02E 325 025 AS** and clutch carrier **02E 398 029 C**
  [VENDOR redlineautoparts]. Both numbering series appear in circulation
  for MQB DQ250 mechatronics — treat the exact mechatronic PN as
  **must-verify against the gearbox ID plate** before ordering. [Ross-Tech;
  Vdladelaide]

**Caveat:** the individual ratios and the two final drives (4.77 / 3.44)
come from a single enthusiast forum post [FORUM vwvortex]; the
**output-shaft split (1-2-3-4 on shaft 1; 5-6-R on shaft 2, each with its
own final drive) is well-corroborated architecture** [FORUM audi-sport
citing VW technical consultant Mark Fowler; vwvortex]. No official VW spec
sheet tabulating the exact Golf-R ratio set was found. 5th (1.04) reading
numerically higher than 4th (0.97) is normal because 5th runs on the second

comparable across shafts. For sanity only, the Passat 2.0 TDI DQ250 uses
finals 4.118/3.043 [VENDOR gearboxlist] — do NOT use those as Golf-R
numbers. [VW Vortex; gearboxlist]

## Clutch-pack spec block (K1 / K2) [02E-verified; same pack used on 0D9]

- **Type:** dual concentric wet multi-plate. **K1 = odd gears (1/3/5) +
  reverse → outer hollow input shaft; K2 = even gears (2/4/6) → inner solid
  input shaft.** [SSP 308; gearboxlist; maktrans; aboutthecars]
- **Torque rating: BorgWarner rates the DQ250 clutch module at 380 Nm max
  transferable engine torque** (per BorgWarner service-kit datasheet for
  02E141029P/02E141029N: "Max. transferable engine torque [Nm]: 380"). The
  gearbox family is broadly quoted as designed for up to ~350 Nm (up to
  400 Nm on later diesel-uprated builds); tuning files note the factory TCU
  torque limiter sits at ~350 Nm on many stock DQ250 calibrations. [VENDOR
  BorgWarner datasheet; gearboxlist; utpmotorsport]
- **OEM plate counts: 9 friction discs total (4 large K1 / 5 small K2)
  giving 18 friction surfaces; 11 steel plates (5 large K1 / 6 small K2).**
  APR's published spec states verbatim "OEM: 9 discs & 18 surfaces (4 large
  (K1) / 5 small (K2))" and "OEM: 11 plates (5 large (K1) / 6 small
  (K2))". Independently corroborated by Raybestos Powertrain module
  RCPDCT-250: "6 K2 Steels, 5 Dual Assembly K2 GPZ Frictions, 5 K1 Steels,
  4 Dual Assembly K1 GPZ Frictions." [VENDOR APR/goapr; Raybestos]
- **Friction material:** OEM paper/composite wet-clutch friction;
  performance vendors substitute higher-energy/directional materials.
  [VENDOR APR; SSP]
- **Clutch/carrier housing:** OEM basket **02E 398 029 C** (also 029B); NOT
  interchangeable with early 02E 398 029 / 029A baskets. [VENDOR
  langwieser, redlineautoparts]
- **Shim/clearance kit:** OEM shim set **02E 398 321** — clutch stack must
  be shimmed to spec, then TCU clutch adaptation run. [VENDOR APR/vwvortex]
- **Complete BorgWarner clutch pack (module): 02E 141 029 N** and
  **02E 141 029 P** are the current BorgWarner service modules; a
  friction-only lining set is also sold. VW sells the pack via the module
  number; LuK/Sachs supply aftermarket equivalents; Raybestos RCPDCT-250 is
  a US friction+steel kit. [VENDOR darksidedevelopments, borgwarner,
  raybestos]
- **PEEK sealing-ring kit** for the double clutch: **02E 323 557 B**
  (4 rings). [VENDOR maktrans]
- **Failure modes:** low-speed **shudder/judder on drive-off** (degraded
  fluid + worn friction, commonly the K1/odd pack that also does reverse
  launches); **burnt/glazed K1**; hard/jerky 1–2 engagement; slip under
  load, especially on remapped cars. Fluid change + clutch adaptation fixes
  early shudder; worn packs need replacement + adaptation. [VENDOR
  ecutesting, aboutthecars; FORUM vwaudiforum]

## Mechatronic (J743) subsection

- **Unit:** integrated valve body + TCU (J743) submerged in DSG fluid on
  top of the box. Controls clutch pressure, gear selection, and
  cooling/lube flow. [SSP; aboutthecars]
- **Solenoid map (real DQ250 designations):**
  - **N215** – pressure-control valve 1, **clutch K1** apply pressure.
    [VENDOR maktrans, eco-torque]
  - **N216** – pressure-control valve 2, **clutch K2** apply pressure.
    [same]
  - **N217** – pressure-control valve 3, main-flow/system pressure
    regulation. [VENDOR maktrans]
  - **N218** – pressure-control valve 4; **N233** – pressure-control valve
    5. [VENDOR 4x4parte, controlunits]
  - **N88–N92** – shift solenoid valves 1–5 (fork actuation). [VENDOR
    eco-torque, controlunits]
  - **G509** – clutch temperature monitoring; input/output speed sensors on
    shaft ends; individual **gear-fork position sensors** (one per fork).
    [VENDOR eco-torque; maktrans]
- **Pressure accumulator:** the DQ250 mechatronic contains a pressure
  accumulator that is a known **cracking failure**, giving pressure-loss
  faults and limp behaviour (frequently cited on remapped S3/Golf R
  "cracked gearbox/mechatronic" cases). [FORUM volkswagenforum SKX thread;
  ecutesting]
- **Known DTCs:** P0746/17130 (N215), P0776/17160 (N216), P1814/P1815
  (N215), P1818–P1820 (N216), P1823–P1825 (N217), P1828–P1830 (N218),
  P1833–P1835 (N233); P0731/P0733 gear-incorrect-ratio; P2711/19143
  unexpected mechanical gear disengagement; P1740/18148 clutch-temp (G509);
  P1604 ECU; P175F/P1701 basic-settings-not-performed / after replacement;
  PRNDS flashing + limp (safety solenoid drops a clutch: lose K1 → drive in
  2nd only; lose K2 → drive in 1st/3rd). [VENDOR 4x4parte, eco-torque,
  controlunits; RT wiki; FORUM ross-tech]
- **Mechatronic PN revisions / interchange:** many supersessions
  (02E 927 770 series … AD/AE/AJ/AL/AM/AN/AO/AQ/AS/AT). MQB (UDS)
  mechatronics are software-specific to the gearbox code — a non-matching
  unit must be reflashed to the car's ratios/config or it pops out of gear.
  PQ-based (non-MQB) DQ250 mechatronics do NOT drop straight into an MQB
  0D9 box. [FORUM mhhauto, controlunits]
- **Repair economics:** solenoid-only service (N215/N216 etc.) is cheapest
  but risky if fluid is contaminated; specialist refurb of the mechatronic
  is mid-cost; a genuine exchange box runs to several thousand pounds
  (owners quote ~£5k for a genuine exchange unit). Clutch-pack replacement
  + adaptation is a separate, common job (specialists report doing "a lot
  of DQ250 clutch packs" but few DQ250 mechatronics). [FORUM
  volkswagenforum, vwaudiforum]

## PTO / bevel-box subsection (gearbox-end angle drive) [0D9]

- **Function:** 90° angle drive bolted to the transaxle; turns drive
  rearward to the propshaft/Haldex. Distinct from the Haldex (later brief).
  [VENDOR autoparts-24; FORUM r32oc]
- **Unit part-number family: 0CN 409 053-series** for the MQB Gen-5-Haldex
  Golf R (e.g. 0CN409053AF / 0CN409053AG / 0CN409053AN). The **0CQ409053**
  family the brief cited was NOT confirmed for this car — the confirmed
  transverse-MQB bevel-box family is **0CN 409 053**. [VENDOR vdladelaide
  (0CN409053AF/AN on Mk7 Golf R/S3), autoparts-24 (0CN409053AG, Gen-5
  Haldex MQB)] **Flag: 0CQ vs 0CN discrepancy — use 0CN for Mk7 R.**
- **Oil spec + capacity: G 052 145 S2** (75W-90 GL-5 synthetic; "S2"/"A2"
  only denote bottle size), **~0.9 litre** fill; drain + fill plugs
  present, so it **is serviceable** even though VW's schedule says a
  bevel-box oil change is "not normally required." Many owners service it
  at ~60k miles / double the Haldex interval. Note conflicting VW info
  exists on whether the front bevel box shares rear-diff oil vs Haldex oil
  — VW dealer VIN lookups confirm **same as rear diff = G052145S2**. [FORUM
  ttforum, vwvortex; VENDOR blauparts, shopdap, parts.vw.com]
- **Internals:** hypoid/bevel crown-and-pinion set, taper bearings, input
  shaft (DSG-length differs from manual-box unit), output flange to prop.
  [FORUM r32oc]
- **Failure modes: oil leak from output/rear flange seal and side seals**
  (rear seal notorious — flange corrosion + stuck taper race), and
  **bevel-box whine**; DSG-length input shaft means the correct unit must
  be fitted. [FORUM r32oc, vwroc bevel-box-failure]
- **Plugs:** drain/fill plug **N 90 281 802** (used on bevel box and rear
  diff), marked "renew" with a torque callout. [FORUM ttforum]

## Service-spec block [02E/0D9]

- **DSG oil spec: G 052 182 A2** (within the G052182 family; "A2" = 1 L
  bottle) — the genuine wet-DSG fluid for DQ250/DQ500/DL501; equivalent to
  Pentosin FFL-2. [VENDOR darksidedevelopments, akstuning; okcvw]
- **Oil filter: 02E 305 051 C** (DQ250 6-speed external cartridge).
  Filter-housing O-ring required. NOTE: DQ500/DQ381 use different filters
  (0BH325183B / 0GC-series) — do NOT cross-fit. [VENDOR ebay allparts,
  strperformance]
- **Capacity: total fill ~7.0–7.2 L; service (partial) fill ~5.5 L**
  (ElsaWin figure cited as 5.7 L; "6 L per service" commonly quoted).
  [VENDOR gearboxlist (7.2 total / 5.5 partial); FORUM ttforum (ElsaWin
  5.7)]
- **Level-setting procedure:** overflow method via the level tube — engine
  idling, **fluid temperature 35–45 °C (do not exceed 45 °C)**; open the
  fill/level plug: a trickle = correct, a steady stream = overfull (let it
  drop to a trickle), nothing = add until it trickles. Requires VCDS/ODIS
  to read live DSG oil temp. [VENDOR blauparts; FORUM ttforum]
- **UK service interval: DSG oil + filter every 40,000 miles (≈4 years)
  for the Mk7 6-speed** (the later 7-speed DQ381 is 80k). Typical UK dealer
  price ~£199. Haldex separately every 3 years. **At ~92k miles this car is
  due its 3rd DSG service; a missed 40k service history is the single
  biggest reliability red flag** because degraded fluid is the leading
  cause of shudder and solenoid wear. [FORUM vwroc DSG-service; VENDOR
  shopdap, mountune]

## Adaptation / basic-settings section (VCDS/ODIS, MQB UDS)

- This is a **UDS-protocol MQB box (Component "DQ250-6A MQB")**, addressed
  by Ross-Tech as the **0D9 DSG**. Because it is UDS, the old "measuring
  block 08" groups are not directly accessible; procedures use advanced
  measuring values / basic-setting routines. [RT wiki; FORUM ross-tech]
- **Basic-setting group sequence (VCDS, DQ250):** run through **groups 061,
  060, 062, 067, 068, 065, 063, 069** with the brake pressed, engine warm,
  outside-air-temp readable. Named routines: **clutch adaptation** (group
  062 for SW <0800, group **067 for SW ≥0800**), **reset values clutch
  safety function** (068), **reset values pressure adaptation** (065),
  **steering-wheel paddle installation** (063). [VENDOR vag-coding,
  dodsonmotorsport calibration PDF; FORUM tuningtechnics]
- **Defined adaptation drive (test drive):** after basic settings, perform
  a staged drive — accelerate through the gears in Drive (not Tiptronic),
  clutch-calibration RPM window ~1200–3500 rpm, one sharp braking followed
  by a short full-throttle pull (oil-return check), evaluate creep/take-off
  points, then re-scan for faults. [WSM/VENDOR dodsonmotorsport; SSP
  disassembly manual]
- **When required:** after **clutch-pack replacement (mandatory — skipping
  it can destroy the new plates)**, after **mechatronic/gearbox
  replacement** (which sets P175F/P1701 until basic settings complete), and
  it is good practice after a DSG oil service if shift quality changed.
  [VENDOR APR/dodson; RT wiki P1701; FORUM ross-tech P175F]
- **"DSG reset" myth vs reality:** the throttle-pedal "DSG reset" ritual
  circulated on forums is **not the official procedure**; the correct
  action is a **VCDS/ODIS basic-setting (adaptation reset) + defined test
  drive** as above. Ross-Tech documents only the basic-setting/adaptation
  route. Treat pedal-dance "resets" as folklore. [RT wiki; VENDOR
  vag-coding]
- **Relevant measuring values:** clutch temperatures (G509), clutch
  engagement/kiss points, clutch pressures, fork positions, input/output
  shaft speeds. On UDS a failed basic setting may read as an intermediate
  percentage (e.g. 192→96) rather than a clean pass. [FORUM ross-tech]

## Recommendations (staged, for the ~92k-mile car)

1. **Immediately verify the gearbox code** off the ID plate (expect
   PUR/QSP/PZS) and pull a **full VCDS/ODIS auto-scan** of address 02 —
   record HW/SW (expect HW 02E 927 770-xx or 02E 325 025-xx, SW 0D9 300
   0xx, Component DQ250-6A MQB) and any stored P07xx/P17xx/P175F/P2711
   codes. This governs every clutch/mechatronic part you'd order for the
   model.
2. **Service the DSG now if there is any doubt about the 40k history.** At
   92k the car should be on its 3rd change (40/80/120k schedule puts it
   just past due). Use **G 052 182 A2 + filter 02E 305 051 C + housing
   O-ring**, ~5.5 L service fill, set level by overflow at 35–45 °C with a
   live temp readout.
3. **If shudder/judder on drive-off is present:** first change fluid and
   **run the VCDS clutch adaptation (basic setting group 067 for SW ≥0800)
   + defined test drive**. If shudder persists, plan a **BorgWarner clutch
   module (02E 141 029 N/P) + shim kit (02E 398 321)** replacement —
   adaptation is then mandatory.
4. **Service the bevel box** (G 052 145 S2, ~0.9 L, plug N90281802
   renewed) — cheap insurance against the known rear-seal leak/whine;
   inspect the output-flange area for corrosion.
5. **Inspect mounts** (5Q0 199 555 gearbox mount + 5Q0 199 855 dogbone) —
   worn dogbone bushes mimic "gearbox" clunk/vibration and are cheap.
6. **Before any clutch/mechatronic parts order,** confirm Nm+angle for
   mount bolts, gearbox-to-engine bolts and driveshaft-flange bolts from an
   **erWin/Bentley 0D9 torque table** (marked NONE FOUND here).
7. **Thresholds that change the plan:** stored **pressure-control
   (N215/N216) or accumulator-related pressure DTCs, or
   PRNDS-flashing/limp** → mechatronic refurb/replace (with basic settings)
   rather than just fluid; **P0731/P0733 incorrect-ratio or P2711
   gear-disengagement** → internal/synchro or fork-sensor investigation;
   **metallic debris in drained fluid** → full teardown decision, not a
   fluid change; **any engine remap** pushing past the **BorgWarner 380 Nm
   clutch rating** → upgraded clutch pack + TCU tune before the OEM pack
   slips/cracks.

## Caveats

This report blends **02E-architecture facts (SSP 308)** with
**0D9/Golf-R-specific catalogue and forum data**; where a part number is
02E-catalogued it is noted, and 02E and 0D9 part numbers frequently differ
even when the architecture is identical. Several fasteners and torque
figures are deliberately marked NONE FOUND rather than guessed.
Enthusiast-forum figures (gear ratios, some torques, repair costs) are
flagged as such and should be confirmed against an erWin/Bentley 0D9
workshop manual and the physical gearbox ID plate before any parts are
ordered or any model geometry is finalised. The most robustly verified
structural facts for the exploded model are: the **K1(odd+R)/K2(even)
wet-clutch split with 9 friction / 11 steel OEM plate counts**, the
**380 Nm BorgWarner clutch rating**, the **two-output-shaft /
two-final-drive layout (1-2-3-4 vs 5-6-R)**, the **four
hydraulically-actuated selector forks each with its own position sensor**,
and the **integrated mechatronic (N215/N216 clutch regulators +
N217/N218/N233 pressure + N88–N92 shift solenoids)**.
