# R11 return — Body, exterior & interior trim: 2014 pre-facelift UK RHD VW Golf R Mk7 (5-door, 5G)

## TL;DR

The pre-facelift 5-door UK Golf R shares its core body-in-white, door shells,
front wings and bonnet with the GTI/standard Golf (5G0/5G4 panels), and is
differentiated only by bolt-on cosmetic parts: R-specific front bumper
(5G0807221BJ), rear bumper (5G6807421 family) + gloss-black quad-exhaust
diffuser (5G6807568AG041), matt/satin-chrome mirror caps (5G0072530A), R roof
spoiler lip (5G6827940A041), R upper grille with matt-aluminium strip
(5G0853651E ADQ), quad-exhaust valance, R flat-bottom wheel (5G0419091
family), "Carbon Touch" decor and dark-tinted dynamic-LED rear clusters.

**The R10 rear-light gap is now CLOSED:** the pre-FL R/GTI use the
dynamic-turn-signal LED set — outer 5G0945207G (L) / 5G0945208G (R), inner
5G0945307P/Q (L) / 5G0945308P/Q (R), full VW kit 5G1052200C; the flip
VW-emblem reversing camera is 5G0827469F.

**Paint:** the UK pre-FL R offered **Pure White** (solid, L-code **LC9A**,
sticker **0Q0Q** — the standard solid white) and **Oryx White Mother-of-Pearl**
(premium pearl, L0K1, sticker **0R0R**); **Candy White was NOT an R colour**
(lesser Golfs only). The data sticker lives under the plastic trim over the
lower boot/tailgate latch (also spare-well area) and is duplicated on page 1
of the service book.

## Key Findings

**Part-number prefix logic (Mk7 5G) — verified against dealer catalogues +
breakers:**

- **5G0** = general/shared body & exterior (bonnet, wings, bumper covers,
  grilles, mirror caps, tail-light outer bodies) — used across 3-door,
  5-door, GTI, R.
- **5G1** = LHD-specific cockpit/wiper/loom (LHD dash carrier, LHD wiper
  arms/linkage, LHD bonnet release cable); also some assembly kits (LED tail
  kit 5G1052200C).
- **5G2 = RHD-specific** cockpit/wiper/loom (RHD dash, RHD wiper
  arms/linkage/motor, RHD bonnet release cable, RHD knee airbag 5G2880842D).
  **The key flag for this UK car.**
- **5G3** = 3-door-specific panels/trim (e.g. 3-door door graphics
  5G3853335B). [VW Vortex]
- **5G4 = 5-door-specific** panels/trim (rear door trims 5G4853417/418; door
  lock 5G4837085 family; 5-door front seatbelts 5G4857705B/706). **Correct
  prefix for this car's rear doors + their trim.** [VW Vortex]
- **5G6** = tailgate/hatch-mounted items (tailgate shell, rear bumper cover
  5G6807421, diffuser 5G6807568, roof spoiler 5G6827940, tailgate struts
  5G6827550, rear wiper blade 5G6955427E).
- **5GM / 5G9** = estate/Variant (SportWagen) — flag if a PN verifies only
  against these; NOT the hatch.
- **Suffix logic:** trailing letters = revision level; a following 3-char
  code denotes colour/finish — GRU = grey primer/unpainted, 041 = gloss
  black, 3Q7/ADQ = matt aluminium, FOD = specific finish, SZLL/CSZLL =
  grille variants.

**R-SPECIFIC ITEMS (everything that differs from a GTI/base Golf):** front
bumper cover + lower grille set with matt-alu trims; upper grille with
matt-alu strip + R badge; matt/satin-chrome mirror caps; R roof spoiler lip;
R side skirts; rear bumper + gloss-black quad-exhaust diffuser; dark-tinted
dynamic-LED rear clusters; R flat-bottom steering wheel with chrome "R"
lower-spoke logo; R instrument cluster (blue needles / R logo); "Carbon
Touch" dash & door decor + blue ambient lighting; blue-illuminated R sill
guards; stainless R pedal caps; R sports seats (cloth or Vienna leather)
with R logos; black headliner.

## How this system works / how it's built (narrative)

The Mk7 Golf is built on VW's **MQB (Modularer Querbaukasten)** platform. Per
Self-Study Programme **SSP 520 "The Golf 2013 – Body and Occupant
Protection"** and VW's engineering release, the **body-in-white weighs
exactly 23 kg less than the Mk6's**, broken down by VW as **−12 kg** from
advanced/high-strength steel grades and reduced sheet thickness, **−4 kg**
from tailor-rolled blanks ("only using materials where they are needed"),
and **−7 kg** from optimised profile and surface geometries. At vehicle
level the theoretical maximum saving is **109 kg** (up to −6.0 kg
electrical, −40.0 kg engines, −26.0 kg running gear, −37.0 kg
superstructure), with **100 kg the maximum achieved in any one variant**.

The safety cell relies heavily on **hot-formed ultra-high-strength steel**:
the **high-strength-steel share rose from 66% (Mk6) to 80%**, and the
specifically **hot-formed ultra-high-strength content grew from 6% to 28% of
the body**. Those hot-formed parts (A/B-pillars, roof frame, tunnel, sill
zones) have a **tensile yield strength of ~1,000 MPa — over six times
conventional deep-drawn steel** — formed from a blank heated to **~950 °C**
then quenched in the die. New joining technology debuted too, notably the
**laser clamp welder producing "wobble welds"** (a sinusoidal laser seam on
short flanges) in the side structure. Crash energy is routed from the bumper
crash beams through the longitudinals and into this hot-formed cell.

**Bolt-on vs welded:** the bonnet and front wings (bolt-on), doors and
tailgate are replaceable closures; the roof, sills/rockers and rear quarters
are **welded structural** — body-shop items, hence NONE FOUND service part
numbers. **The Golf R shares its BIW, doors, wings and bonnet with the
GTI/base Golf** (VW's own catalogue lists front wing 5G0821105B fitting
Golf, GTI, R, Alltrack, e-Golf and SportWagen); differentiation is achieved
entirely with **bolt-on cosmetics and interior trim, not sheet metal**. The
R's front bumper has a unique lower-intake design and, on this ACC car, the
**centre lower grille houses the radar aperture**; the rear bumper carries
the **gloss-black quad-exhaust diffuser interface** (ties to R4).

**RHD-specific assemblies** — dash carrier, wiper arms/linkage/motor, bonnet
release cable, knee airbag — carry the **5G2 prefix** (vs 5G1 for LHD), the
single most important market flag for this UK car. Finally, the **4Motion
driveline raises the boot floor over the Haldex rear-drive unit**, so the R
uses a specific higher boot-floor trim (5G6863459 family) with its own
spare-wheel tool foam. Even the seats contributed to the diet: VW cut **up
to 7 kg (>15%) from front and rear seats**, mainly lighter rear-backrest
latch mechanisms.

## Recommendations

1. **Model the verified R differentiators first** — they are all
   catalogue/vendor-confirmed and define the car visually: front bumper
   5G0807221BJ, upper grille 5G0853651E ADQ, matt-chrome mirror caps
   5G0072530A, roof spoiler 5G6827940A041, diffuser 5G6807568AG041, tail
   set 5G0945207G/208G + 5G0945307/308 + camera 5G0827469F.
2. **Default paint = Pure White (LC9A / sticker 0Q0Q)** to match the target
   car; offer Oryx White pearl (L0K1 / 0R0R) and Lapiz Blue (LD5K) as
   premium variants — these three anchor the R's identity.
3. **Tag every 5G2 part as RHD-specific** in the app metadata (wiper arms
   5G2955409/410B, wiper linkage/motor 5G2955023C, bonnet latch 5G2823509G,
   bonnet cable 5G2823531D, knee airbag 5G2880842D) so the UK build is
   distinguishable from US/LHD.
4. **Mark structural panels (roof, quarters, sills, crash beams) as
   welded/body-shop with NONE FOUND** rather than inventing numbers.
5. **Threshold to upgrade the data:** if erWin/ETKA access is obtained,
   replace all "family/NONE FOUND" door-shell, glass, and rear-motor PNs
   with exact 5G4/5G6 numbers and capture hinge/striker + seat-rail torques
   verbatim — at that point promote the seat-rail (~25 Nm) and belt
   (40/23 Nm) figures from "MQB analog" to "verified."

## Caveats & source disagreements / open flags

- **Exact PNs NONE FOUND** for: door shells (5G4 family only), all glass,
  roof/quarters, number-plate lamps, CHMSL, complete mirror units,
  indicator repeaters, pedal caps, rear sill guards, gear-selector
  surround, parcel shelf, front/side airbags (module PNs), crash-sensor
  satellites.
- **Rear wiper motor PN unverified** for the hatch (aftermarket cross-refs
  cite 5K6955711B/C — treat as unconfirmed).
- **Safety torques** (belt latch/anchor 40 Nm, height-adjuster 23 Nm, guide
  screws 1.5 Nm, seat-rail ~25 Nm) come from the MQB restraint family /
  cross-platform analog — **verify against 5G erWin before treating as
  authoritative** (belt figures are more solid than the seat-rail figure).
- **Rear bumper cover PN is a family** (5G6807421 / superseded 5G6807417
  AP/AR/BA/BQ) — R vs base differ by finish + cutouts; confirm the exact R
  suffix.
- **Two task-supplied PNs were incorrect and corrected** (not invented):
  front wings are 5G0821105B/106B (not 5G0821021/022); bonnet base
  5G0823031 is correct, production suffix -J.
- **Paint codes** are consistent across touch-up vendors
  (paintref/paintscratch), the VW Vortex colour booklet and owner
  service-book readings, but a **period VW UK brochure/configurator archive
  would be the ideal primary confirmation**; the sticker-code pairings
  (0Q0Q, 0R0R, etc.) come from a VW Vortex owner list.
- **Front blade size:** the 650/450 mm (26"/18") Bosch A864S set is the
  well-established R/GTI fitment; a minority of listings quote 700 mm for
  the driver blade — treat 650/450 as primary.
- **US catalogue = 4-door SportWagen / Golf R** with different
  lighting/bumper details; any PN verified only against US/LHD or GTI is
  flagged in-row.
- **Carried R1–R10 open flags remain open** (cam-carrier torque, DQ250
  mechatronic PN, ACC radar exact suffix, etc.) — not addressed in this
  brief per scope.
